<div>Going to campus, SF or the airport soon? Parking and Uber too expensive?<br />
<br />
A Haas student built <b>Poolsy</b>, a simple way to match with another Berkeley student going the same way at a similar time, and split the cost.<br />
<br />
- Save up to 50% on your ride<br />
- Match with students only<br />
- Exact pickup and dropoff<br />
<br />
<u>How it works:</u><br />
1. Post your ride in about 30 seconds<br />
2. Get matched with another student going your way<br />
3. Coordinate and book the ride together<br />
<br />
Try it out at <a data-saferedirecturl="https://www.google.com/url?q=https://haas.campusgroups.com/click?email_links_id%3D399079%26member_id%3D26193431&amp;source=gmail&amp;ust=1788279655718000&amp;usg=AOvVaw1CBWnZOr3bXnoVhVoOSQlZ" href="https://haas.campusgroups.com/click?email_links_id=399822&member_id=26205835" target="_blank">https://ridepoolsy.com</a>, and share with classmates who may need it!<br />
<br />
Great for campus commutes, airport trips, SF weekends, and late night rides.</div>
